AMD DROP-37 READER: EVIDENCE AND INTERPRETATION — 20 OCTOBER 2026

Target and terminology
The reader targets the independently numerically resolved population
density-mode count in simulations. FCM configurations are clusters of the
fitted partition at a specified resolution and data representation. They
are distinct from generating components, modes and modal basins. Correct
count does not validate observation assignments or substantive meaning.
One density mode is not absence of all structure.

Among 565 reference values >=0.9, 50 exact-count errors occurred. A
post-result subgroup of 189 proposals of K=2 contained 46 errors; all five
forests agreed in those errors. This descriptive cutoff is not a K rule.

Operational consequence
The package retains modal-count estimation. It reports proposed_k and
proposed_multiple_modes, forest agreement, and continuous conditional
simulation-reference values separately. Reliability for the distribution
being analysed is not established by N,D membership, high reference
confidence or forest unanimity. No automatic applicability threshold is
claimed. allow_extrapolation exposes reference values; it cannot validate
transport. The AMD peak is a numerical summary, not an automatic K rule.
